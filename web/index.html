01 · CONFIGURED FROM YOUR ADMIN

Change your mint from its admin page.

Fees, the cap on liquidity opening fees, your domain: you set them in your mint's admin UI, and Run rolls the change out for you. There are no config files to edit and no servers to log into.

  • Point and click. No YAML, no terminal, no cloud console.
  • Applied for you. Run rolls each change out and stops the node gracefully first.
  • On record. Every change is kept in Run's history, so nothing changes without a trace.
  • Isolated. Each mint gets its own node, volume and keys.

02 · LIQUIDITY THAT MANAGES ITSELF

Inbound capacity arrives with the first payment.

A new Lightning node can't be paid, because nobody has a channel to it. Run's mints use LSPS2, the open standard for just-in-time channels. When a payment comes in that no channel can carry, the liquidity provider opens one right then and forwards the payment over it. The opening fee comes out of that payment.

  • No bootstrap ritual. Your first channel doesn't need on-chain coins or a peer you know.
  • Capped fees. Every mint has a ceiling on opening fees, written in its manifest, and quotes are checked against it.
  • Grows with demand. Bigger payments bring bigger channels. Idle capacity isn't paid for up front.
  • Standard, not proprietary. LSPS2 is supported by ldk-node, and any compliant LSP can be swapped in.
  1. t+0s
    A wallet pays your Lightning Address

    The invoice routes to the LSP, which holds the payment.

  2. t+1s
    The LSP opens a channel to your mint

    It is zero-conf and sized to the payment with headroom to spare, and the opening fee is checked against your cap.

  3. t+2s
    The payment is forwarded and claimed

    Your mint claims it, minus the opening fee.

  4. t+2s
    A note is minted

    The payer holds an LNURLcash bearer note. The channel stays for the next one.

03 · CHAIN SYNC, YOUR CHOICE

Our full node by default. Any Electrum server if you prefer.

A Lightning node needs to watch the chain: it has to see its channels confirm, catch a cheating counterparty, and sweep closed channels home. Out of the box, your mint syncs from Run's own fully validating Bitcoin node. If you'd rather trust another source, point it at a public Electrum server or one you run yourself.

  • Works out of the box. Run's full node is the default, with nothing to set up.
  • Bring your own. Any Electrum server, public or your own, set from the admin.
  • Nothing to download. Your mint never syncs the blockchain itself.

04 · TOTALLY SELF-MANAGING

The operations a node needs, done for you.

Upgrades, rolled out

New lnurl-mint releases are rolled out for you, and the node is stopped gracefully first, so channel state is written before it restarts.

Snapshots of node state

Your node's seed, channel monitors and notes live on a persistent volume, and that volume is snapshotted on a schedule. A Lightning node's state is precious, and these backups are designed with that in mind.

Health that heals

Liveness checks and automatic restarts are built in. On restart the mint reconciles: a pending melt is finished, resent or released, and never guessed at.

Routing that knows the map

Rapid Gossip Sync loads the Lightning network graph on boot, so melts route from the first minute.

Your domain, with TLS

Your mint lives at you.run.lnurlcash.com, or on your own domain, with certificates issued and renewed automatically.

The full admin, still yours

The mint's admin UI and lnurl-mint-cli are included: balances, channels, notes, payouts. Self-managing never means locked out.

05 · YOURS TO LEAVE

No lock-in. It's the same open-source mint.

A Run mint is lnurl-mint-rust, unmodified and MIT-licensed. Export its data directory, which holds the seed, the node state and the notes, and start it on your own hardware: 

$ docker run -d --network host -v ./data:/data --env-file .env \
    lnurlcash/lnurl-mint-rust

The same notes. The same node, with its channels open. Only your host changes.

Questions

Who holds the keys?

During the beta, your mint's node runs on Run's infrastructure, and its seed lives on your mint's own volume there. You run the admin, can export the whole data directory, and can move to your own machine at any time. If you want the seed never to leave your hardware, run lnurl-mint-rust yourself. It's the same software.

Is an LNURLcash mint custodial?

Yes. A bearer note is a claim on the mint that issued it, the way a banknote is a claim on its issuer. That's why the mint is open source, its notes carry certificates, and every spend is checked by Bitcoin Core's own script interpreter. Read the LNURLcash spec for the details.

Which network?

Bitcoin mainnet, with a signet option for trying things out without real sats.

What does it cost?

The beta is free. There will be a plan for running mints, plus LSPS2 opening fees when your mint needs inbound capacity. Those fees are paid out of incoming payments, never by surprise.

When?

When it's ready, and not before. Lightning nodes hold money, so the beta opens in small batches. Join the list and you'll hear from us on nostr.
